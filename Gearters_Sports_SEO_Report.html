Rule A: Product Titles (Names)

Keep titles descriptive and focused on what the item actually is. The website automatically tags "Gearters Sports" at the end for Google, so do not type "Gearter" in front of the name.

❌ Do Not Use:

✓ Best Practice:

Tip: Always ensure the Article Number (e.g., GS-1003, S-0001) is typed accurately.

Rule B: Product Descriptions (Crucial for Ranking)

Please avoid 1-sentence descriptions. Aim for 80 to 120 words using this 4-point structure:

  • Material & Padding: Genuine cowhide leather, 4-way stretch polyester, multi-density EVA foam.
  • Fit & Closure: Full wrap-around Velcro strap, lace-up closure, elastic drawstring waistband.
  • Intended Usage: Pro sparring, heavy bag drills, amateur bouts, competition.
  • B2B Wholesale Line: "Available for custom brand logos, OEM private labeling, and bulk export orders worldwide."
Sample Recommended Description:
"The Pro Competition Gloves (Art No. GS-1003) are engineered for professional sparring and fight conditioning. Handcrafted from premium cowhide leather with multi-layered shock-absorbing EVA foam for optimal knuckle protection and wrist alignment. Features a secure lace-up closure and breathable lining. Built for gym owners, fight clubs, and retail sports brands. Full OEM custom branding, private labeling, and bulk export available worldwide."

Rule C: Product Images

Use crisp, clean photos on solid or transparent backgrounds. When possible, add 2–3 images showing different angles (front, back, closure/wristband) so international buyers can inspect stitching and craftsmanship.
